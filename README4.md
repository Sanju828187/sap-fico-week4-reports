# SAP FICO Week 4 Reports

## Internship Project – Week 4

This repository contains the Week 4 SAP FICO internship deliverable on **SAP FICO Reports**.

### Contents
- `Week_4_SAP_FICO_Reports.docx` – detailed internship report
- `.gitignore` – excludes common temporary, operating-system, editor and Office files

### Report Coverage
The report explains:
- SAP FICO reporting overview
- General Ledger and financial statement reporting
- Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable reports
- Asset Accounting reports
- Cost Centre and Profit Centre reports
- Internal Order reporting
- Practical reporting workflow
- Real-world business scenarios
- Business benefits
- Key observations and learning

### Practical Focus
The report connects SAP FICO report types with practical finance questions such as:
- What is the current account balance?
- Which vendor invoices are outstanding?
- Which customers have overdue receivables?
- Where are costs being incurred?
- Which business area requires further analysis?
- What transactions explain a significant financial variance?

### Repository Purpose
This repository is maintained as part of the SAP FICO internship Week 4 submission and is intended to demonstrate structured documentation, practical understanding and clear presentation of SAP FICO reporting concepts.
